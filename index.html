Great ideas begin with asking the right questions.

I am a researcher and engineer working on multi-energy systems. My training is in mechanical and thermal-fluid engineering, with a PhD in Industrial Engineering from the University of Naples Federico II. My current work connects physical models with data.

I work on digital twins, optimization and machine learning for micro gas turbines, hydrogen storage, heat pumps with thermal storage, and building energy systems. I also build the software around them: solvers, sensor-data pipelines and monitoring platforms.

I care about models that are validated against measured data and that other people can run.
