01 — About

Great ideas begin with asking the right questions.

I'm a researcher, engineer and perpetual problem-solver. For years I've worked on multi-energy systems, turning complex energy challenges into designs that cut costs, emissions and inefficiency.

From AI-driven optimization to hydrogen hubs, the thread is the same: merge technology and impact, and craft a solution that not only works but inspires. The energy world keeps evolving, and I'm here to help it evolve, one optimized system at a time.
